      How the scores are worked out

      IoU (intersection over union) measures how well a box lines up with a true difference: the overlapping area divided by the area the two boxes cover together. 1.0 is a perfect fit, 0 is no overlap.

      Each box can be paired with at most one difference. A difference counts as found when a box overlaps it with an IoU of at least . A box that isn’t paired with any difference is a wrong box.

      Accuracy = found ÷ (found + missed + wrong boxes). Mean IoU averages, over every true difference, the IoU of its paired box, or of the closest box if it was missed. Precision = found ÷ all boxes. Recall = found ÷ all differences.

      When an icon moves, its old spot and its new spot are scored as two separate differences.

      The AI models are scored with exactly the same rules. A model marks a changed icon once in each picture; when both of its boxes are on the same spot they are merged into one box covering both, the way a player draws one box.
